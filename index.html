How it works

  • Add task or N: new box
  • Drag from the right dot onto another box: that box waits for it
  • Click an arrow, then Delete: remove the dependency
  • Click a box: edit it and light up what it waits for (dark) and unblocks (blue)
  • Shift-click: add or remove a box from the selection
  • Shift-drag on the background: select a region
  • Drag a selected box: move the whole selection
  • Select its whole chain: grab a full tree to move it
  • Drag the background, scroll: pan and zoom, F fits the board
  • Tidy layout: re-arrange, and close gaps when done tasks are hidden
  • Hide done: keep finished work out of sight
  • Workflow boards: map a process, with decisions, owners, labelled arrows and loops
  • /: search, Enter jumps to the next match
  • Lanes: one band per group, drag a box between bands to regroup it
  • Colours: colour by meaning, Label colours names them
  • Orange arrows: longest chain of remaining work
  • D: toggle done on the selected box
  • Esc: deselect
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Z: undo, Shift+Z redo
  • Ctrl+Enter in Update from text: propose changes
